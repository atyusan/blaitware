#### Why join us

- Ship real SaaS used by businesses internationally
- Pair product work with meaningful customer delivery
- Collaborate across CRM, Workplace, hosting, healthcare, and education domains

1. Own outcomes for your area—features, implementations, or customer success.
2. Partner with stakeholders to clarify requirements and trade-offs.
3. Document decisions and hand over work cleanly to the next owner.
4. Uphold security, quality, and operational excellence.

#### What we look for

- Relevant experience in your discipline
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Comfort working in iterative delivery with demos and feedback
- Interest in B2B SaaS and technology consulting
