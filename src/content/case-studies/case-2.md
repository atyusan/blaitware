##### Client

Horizon Clinics runs outpatient and inpatient services and needed dependable hospital management software with local implementation support.

##### Challenge

Paper registers and disconnected billing tools caused queue delays, revenue leakage, and poor visibility for administrators.

##### Concept

Deploy Care-Sync for registration, appointments, clinical notes, and billing—delivered by our team with infrastructure guidance tied to Blaitware’s regional presence.

![Case study visual](/images/case-studies/02.png)

##### Solution

- Phased rollout starting with outpatient clinics
- Staff training for front desk, clinicians, and finance
- Reporting pack for utilization and collections
- Hypercare support through the first billing cycle

##### Results

Patient wait visibility improved, billing disputes fell, and leadership gained daily operational dashboards.
