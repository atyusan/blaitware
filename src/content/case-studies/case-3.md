##### Client

Greenfield Academy needed admissions, attendance, results, and fee tracking in one system parents could trust.

##### Challenge

Administrators spent days reconciling fees; parents lacked a reliable portal for progress and payments.

##### Concept

Implement Edu-Core with parent portal access and our change-management support for teachers and bursars.

![Case study visual](/images/case-studies/03.png)

##### Solution

- Digitized student records and class structures
- Enabled fee plans and payment status tracking
- Rolled out teacher attendance and results entry
- Opened parent portal with announcements

##### Results

Fee collection follow-up became proactive, and teachers stopped maintaining parallel grade books.
