##### Client

Atlas Logistics needed an internal portal for shipments and partner status—beyond what off-the-shelf tools offered.

##### Challenge

Ops relied on chat messages and spreadsheets; executives lacked trustworthy KPIs.

##### Concept

We designed and built a custom management system, integrated Blait Analytics dashboards, and hosted the portal with Blaitware infrastructure guidance.

![Case study visual](/images/case-studies/05.png)

##### Solution

- Discovery workshops with dispatch and finance
- Iterative portal delivery with staging demos
- Analytics views for on-time delivery and exceptions
- Handover docs and admin training

##### Results

Dispatchers work from one system of record; weekly exec reviews use live dashboards instead of manual decks.
